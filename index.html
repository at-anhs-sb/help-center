01 WELCOME
WELCOME TO THE HELP CENTER

Need help with the club? You're in the right place. This center contains the basic rules, community guidelines, and useful information for keeping the club organized and enjoyable.

TIP // Read the rules before posting, chatting, or submitting requests.
02 RULES & GUIDELINES
01 // RESPECT

RESPECT EVERY MEMBER

Treat every member with respect. No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or unnecessary hostility.

02 // KEEP IT CLEAN

KEEP DISCUSSIONS CLEAN

Keep discussions appropriate for the club. Avoid spam, flooding, excessive advertising, and disruptive behavior.

03 // CHESS FAIR PLAY

PLAY FAIR

Follow Chess.com's fair-play rules. Do not encourage cheating, engine abuse, account sharing, or other unfair play.

04 // NO DRAMA

HANDLE DISAGREEMENTS CALMLY

Take disagreements calmly. If an issue cannot be resolved, contact the owner instead of turning it into public drama.

05 // STAY ON TOPIC

KEEP THINGS RELEVANT

Keep help requests and club discussions relevant. Use the appropriate area when one is available.

06 // COMMON SENSE

USE COMMON SENSE

Do not exploit loopholes in the rules. Staff may act against behavior that clearly harms the club or its members.
